# Read back

Tell whoever asked you, in your own words, what you think they want. Read back the request you're
working on now. When their later messages changed it, go by the later ones.

- **The goal:** what they want to be true when the work is done.
- **The problem:** what's in the way today, and why it matters to them.
- **What you'd do next,** in a line.

Write it in your own words. Quote only a name they gave a thing. When you're unsure of a part,
including the problem, give your guess, and mark it as a guess.

Then stop, and wait for them to confirm or correct you. When the correction changes the goal or the
problem, read back again. Otherwise take the correction, and do the step you said you'd do next.

**Reply:** the goal, the problem, and what you'd do next, as the three bullets above.
